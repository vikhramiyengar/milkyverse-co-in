Before you write

Questions we
get asked.

What do you need to start?
CAD files or drawings where they exist, otherwise good photographs and measurements. Material and colour references help, and we can build from scratch when nothing exists yet.
Stills or film?
Both come from the same model, so a launch set of stills and a short film together is often the best value.
Can a 3D model live on my website?
Yes. Our own site runs a live watch model you can turn and configure; we build the same for products and spaces.
Who owns the files and the rights?
You do. Final images, films and the agreed source files are yours to use wherever you like.
