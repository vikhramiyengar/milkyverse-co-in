Before you write

Questions we
get asked.

What do you need from me to start?
What the business does, who it is for, any existing brand assets, and examples of sites you like or hate. We take it from there.
Do you write the copy?
Yes. We draft it from the brief and your inputs, and refine it with you until it reads like you.
Can I edit the site myself afterwards?
Yes. We set up the pages and content you will change often so you can update them without touching code, and we document how.
Where is the site hosted and who owns it?
On your own domain and accounts. You own the code, the design files and the hosting. We stay available if you want us to keep looking after it.
Do you work with businesses outside India?
Yes. We are based in India and build for clients anywhere; everything happens over calls and shared links.
