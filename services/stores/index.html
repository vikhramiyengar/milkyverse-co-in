Online stores

A store that looks like a brand and sells like a shop.

A good store is a product page that makes someone want it and a checkout that gets out of the way. We design both, on Shopify or in code, and wire up the tracking so your ads can learn.

or email connect@milkyverse.co.in
Who this is for
  • D2C brands launching or relaunching
  • Jewellery, fashion and lifestyle labels
  • Brands moving off a marketplace
  • Stores that look fine but do not convert
What you get

Theme design and build

A Shopify theme designed for your brand, built to be fast and easy to run. Or a custom storefront when the idea needs it.

Product pages that sell

Imagery, variants, size and material details, trust signals and offers arranged in the order a buyer actually decides.

Checkout for India

UPI, cards, wallets, and cash-on-delivery or partial COD where the business needs it. Fewer steps, fewer drop-offs.

Tracking that works

Meta pixel and Conversions API, Google tags and GA4 set up and verified, so campaigns have real signals to optimise on.

Catalogue and feeds

Collections, filters, product feeds for Meta and Google Shopping, and merchant setup.

Launch support

Stock, shipping zones, policies and emails checked before the first ad goes live.

Before you write

Questions we
get asked.

Shopify or a custom store?
Shopify for almost every brand: it is reliable, you can run it yourself, and the app ecosystem covers most needs. We go custom only when the experience itself is the product.
Can you move my existing store?
Yes. Products, customers, orders and redirects from the old URLs so you keep your search rankings.
Do you set up payments for India?
Yes: Razorpay or Shopify Payments, UPI, cards, wallets and COD or partial COD. We test every method before launch.
Do you run the ads too?
We set up the tracking and can brief or build the creative. Media buying is agreed per project.
