Before you write

Questions we
get asked.

How small can a first version be?
As small as it needs to be to learn something. We help you cut scope to the part that proves the idea, and plan the rest.
iOS, Android or both?
Usually both from one codebase. Where one platform matters more, we release there first and follow with the other.
Who owns the code?
You do. Repositories, accounts and infrastructure are set up in your name from the start.
Do you maintain the app afterwards?
Yes, on a retainer or per release, whichever fits. Monitoring and crash reporting are part of every launch.
